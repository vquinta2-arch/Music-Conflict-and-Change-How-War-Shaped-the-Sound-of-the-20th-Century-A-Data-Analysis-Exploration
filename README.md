# How Did War Shape the Sound of Music? A Data-Driven Exploration

This project explores the relationship between 20th-century global conflicts—specifically World War II, the Korean War, and the Vietnam War—and the evolution of musical characteristics in recorded music from 1936 to 1974.

## Project Overview

Using a Spotify dataset of thousands of tracks, our team analyzed how major geopolitical conflicts influenced structural changes in music production, such as acousticness, instrumentalness, and energy levels. 

The analysis aims to uncover how labor disputes, technological advancements, and shifting cultural sentiments were mirrored in the "musical fingerprints" of the era.

## Data Analysis Approach

Our analysis focused on the following key Spotify metrics:
- **Acousticness**: Confidence measure of a track being acoustic.
- **Instrumentalness**: Predicts whether a track contains no vocals.
- **Vocalness**: Likelihood that a track contains vocals.
- **Energy & Valence**: Measures of intensity and emotional positivity.

We examined these metrics across pre-war, wartime, and post-war eras to identify structural shifts in music production (e.g., the impact of the 1945 AFM ban).
